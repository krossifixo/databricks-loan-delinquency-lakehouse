QUESTION: Which FICO band has the highest average target_delinquent_next in delinquency_training? Return fico_band and the rate.
----------------------------------------

SQL executed:
  SELECT
    `fico_band`,
    ROUND(AVG(`target_delinquent_next`), 4) AS avg_target_delinquent_next
